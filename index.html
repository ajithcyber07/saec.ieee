Preamble

S. A. Engineering College (Autonomous), Chennai – 600077, India is poised to organize the 2027 Third International Conference on Smart Technologies for Sustainable Development Goals (ICSTSDG) in collaboration with IEEE, IEEE Madras Section, IEEE Computer Society Madras Chapter, IEEE Professional Communication Society Madras Chapter & IEEE Circuits and Systems Society Madras Chapter during 04 – 06 August 2027.

About the Conference

The 2027 3rd International Conference on Smart Technologies for Sustainable Development Goals (ICSTSDG) has a special focus on building sustainable solutions. The 2030 agenda for sustainable development agreed by the United Nations member countries aims to improve the quality of life through the 17 core Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) – sdgs.un.org/goals – which have enormous scope for deployment of smart solutions to attain the 169 targets by 2030. ICSTSDG provides a collaborative platform for industry experts and researchers keen to address pressing global challenges by promoting economic growth, social equity and environmental protection, and by supporting innovations that achieve long-term prosperity and protect the planet.

About SAEC

S.A. Engineering College was established by the Dharma Naidu Educational and Charitable Trust in 1998-99. The college is approved by AICTE and was affiliated to Anna University, Chennai; it acquired Autonomous status in 2019-20. With 5 lakh sq.ft of constructed area, it maintains high standards through world-class academic facilities and highly qualified, experienced faculty. M/s TUV NORD has accorded ISO 9001:2015 certification. The college is accredited by NAAC and NBA with 'A' Grade and offers 11 UG and 6 PG programmes.

Paper Submission

Author Guidelines

Papers must be prepared in the standard two-column IEEE conference format (Times New Roman, 10 pt) with the prescribed margins. Full-length papers must not exceed 8 pages including figures and references. Include the title, author names, affiliations and e-mail addresses, an abstract of 150–250 words and 3–5 keywords. Organize the paper into sections such as Introduction, Methodology, Results and Conclusion. Label and cite all figures and tables, number equations, and follow the IEEE numbered citation style. The paper must be original and written in clear, formal English. The manuscript should not contain embedded links, scanned images, headers or footers.

Submission Guidelines

Submitted papers must be original and not under review or published elsewhere. Submit an IEEE-compliant PDF with embedded fonts through the Microsoft CMT system; e-mail submissions are not accepted. Papers are peer reviewed for novelty, technical quality, relevance and clarity, and are subject to the IEEE anti-plagiarism policy. After acceptance, authors revise their paper per reviewer comments and submit the final camera-ready version with the IEEE copyright form. At least one author must register, and accepted papers must be presented to be included in the proceedings and considered for IEEE Xplore, subject to IEEE guidelines.
